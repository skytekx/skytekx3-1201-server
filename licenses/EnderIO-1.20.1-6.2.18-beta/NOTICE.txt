Ender IO 6.2.18-beta for Minecraft 1.20.1 (mods/EnderIO-1.20.1-6.2.18-beta-all.jar)
==================================================================================

The SkyTekx 3 modpack bundles this jar unmodified. It is the release file of Team Ender IO, the same
file as version 6.2.18-beta of the Modrinth project enderio (SHA-1
33587f98f2d96507de0e9d2f57f5ab35328ace7d), built from https://github.com/Team-EnderIO/EnderIO at the
tag v6.2.18-beta (commit 907a9c3721730957fad6794ceed9ed0f591dabac). The jar carries no license file,
so the texts that its parts require travel in this folder.
