Ender IO 6.3.0-skytekx.1 for Minecraft 1.20.1 (mods/EnderIO-1.20.1-6.3.0-skytekx.1-all.jar)
=========================================================================================

The SkyTekx 3 modpack bundles an UNOFFICIAL, MODIFIED build of Ender IO. It is not made, checked or
supported by Team Ender IO. Report problems with it to https://github.com/skytekx/skytekx3-1201-server
and not to Team Ender IO.

The build starts from https://github.com/Team-EnderIO/EnderIO, branch 1.20.1 at commit
18ee1b17bf6879298e911fff751204b07f2e4228 (2026-03-16), which is the release 6.2.18-beta plus two later
community fixes. It adds a subset of the features and fixes of the branch 1.21.1 (Ender IO 8.2.x) up to
commit 8989fcebda3eb9e678fea4138f577c47a22a5e8d. Its complete source is the folder ports/enderio of
https://github.com/skytekx/skytekx3-1201-server at the commit that added the jar to local-mods/, and
ports/enderio/UPSTREAM.md lists the build changes. The jar carries its own change notice as
NOTICE_enderio, the Unlicense as LICENSE_enderio and the texts below in META-INF/licenses/enderio/.
This folder repeats them, so that they travel with every copy of the pack.
