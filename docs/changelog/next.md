## New mods
- Bygone Nether adds Catacombs, Piglin Manors and Citadels to the Nether, eight new mobs, withered
  blackstone, soul stone and the Gilded Netherite Shield. Bastions can now generate in basalt deltas,
  and mined blackstone drops Cobbled Blackstone.
- Mystical Automation adds seven machines for Mystical Agriculture: the Infuser, the Crafter, the
  Farmer, the Fertilizer, the Infusion and Awakening Altarnators and the Enchanternator.
- Mekanism: Extra Modules adds three modules for the MekaSuit body armor. Phase Guard spends suit
  energy to block all damage, Chaos Anchor also stops the laser of the Chaos Guardian while Phase Guard
  runs, and Emergency Revival spends suit energy to cancel one death like a Totem of Undying.
- Expanded Delight adds a cinnamon tree with its wood set, asparagus, sweet potatoes, chili peppers,
  peanuts and cranberries, a juicer, casks that turn milk into cheese wheels, salt and many dishes for
  Farmer's Delight.
- Create: Integrated Farming adds roosts that keep chickens and lay eggs, fishing nets for water and
  lava that also work on contraptions, and the Vacuum Harvester, which harvests and replants the crops
  around it. The Lava Fishing Net catches the fish of Lava Fishing.
- Create: Dragons Plus comes with it and adds liquid dyes, dragon's breath as a fluid that pointed
  dripstone can multiply, the Fluid Hatch and four new kinds of fan processing: Bulk Coloring, Bulk
  Ending, Bulk Freezing and Bulk Sanding. They turn cobblestone into end stone and packed ice into
  blue ice, among other recipes.

## On an existing world
- The structures, blocks and bastions of Bygone Nether only appear in Nether chunks that nobody has
  visited yet, so look beyond the explored area.
- The cinnamon trees, wild crops and salt ore of Expanded Delight only grow in new Overworld chunks.
- The salt of Expanded Delight and of Pam's HarvestCraft unifies to the Mekanism salt.
- Server and client need all six mods, and the pack brings them. Joining with an older pack fails.
