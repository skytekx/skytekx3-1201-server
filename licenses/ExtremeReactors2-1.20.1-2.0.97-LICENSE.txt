Extreme Reactors 2 (ExtremeReactors2-1.20.1-2.0.97.jar) by ZeroNoRyouki declares license="MIT" in its
META-INF/mods.toml. Neither the jar nor the repository https://github.com/ZeroNoRyouki/ExtremeReactors2
carries a license text, so the first notice below is the standard MIT text with the author named in
that mods.toml.

Extreme Reactors 2 continues the 1.7.10 mod Big Reactors. The description in its mods.toml says "This
is a continuation of the 1.7.10 mod Big Reactors from ErogenousBeef", and line 3 of
assets/bigreactors/lang/en_us.json in the jar reads "@author Erogenous Beef, ZeroNoRyouki, ABookFreak".
The reactor and turbine simulation of the jar (ReactorLogic, TurbineLogic and MultiblockReactor under
it/zerono/mods/extremereactors/gamecontent/multiblock/) carries over the code of RadiationHelper,
MultiblockTurbine and MultiblockReactor of Big Reactors, and the fuel column fluid textures
(assets/bigreactors/textures/fluid/fluid.fuelcolumn.still.png and fluid.fuelcolumn.flowing.png) are
Big Reactors files. Big Reactors is MIT licensed, and its license asks that its notice travel with
substantial portions of it, so the second notice below is the LICENSE.txt of
https://github.com/erogenousbeef/BigReactors (branch master) word for word. The README of that
repository gives the years as 2013, 2014.
