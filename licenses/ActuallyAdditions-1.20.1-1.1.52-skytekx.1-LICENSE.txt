Actually Additions 1.20.1-1.1.52-skytekx.1 (unofficial SkyTekx build), license texts
====================================================================================

The jar ActuallyAdditions-1.20.1-1.1.52-skytekx.1.jar in local-mods/ is a modified, unofficial
build of Actually Additions (https://github.com/Ellpeck/ActuallyAdditions) for Minecraft 1.20.1
and Forge 47. Its source with every change is ports/actuallyadditions/ in the SkyTekx repository
(https://github.com/skytekx/skytekx3-1201-server). The jar carries the same texts as
LICENSE_actuallyadditions and NOTICE_actuallyadditions.
