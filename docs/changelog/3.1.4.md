**SkyTekx3 3.1.4 adds Actually Additions, Bygone Nether, Create: Dragons Plus and four mods ported from
NeoForge 1.21.1. Ender IO and Create Crafts & Additions come as SkyTekx builds with the content of their
newer versions, a new fix mod repairs Draconic Evolution, Extreme Reactors, ZeroCore 2 and LittleTiles
move to newer builds, and Apotheosis gets spell casting bosses. Every client needs 3.1.4 to join the
server.**

## Before you join
- Every client needs 3.1.4. Server and client must run the same new mods and the same SkyTekx builds,
  so a client with 3.1.3 or older cannot join.
- You can install 3.1.4 as a new instance or update your 3.1.3 instance. After an update, check that
  the mods folder no longer holds `EnderIO-1.20.1-6.2.18-beta-all.jar`,
  `createaddition-1.20.1-1.3.3.jar`, `LittleTiles_BETA_v1.6.0-pre161_mc1.20.1.jar`,
  `ExtremeReactors2-1.20.1-2.0.84.jar` or `ZeroCore2-1.20.1-2.1.45.jar`, and delete any that are left
  over.

## New mods
- Bygone Nether adds Catacombs, Piglin Manors and Citadels to the Nether, eight new mobs, withered
  blackstone, soul stone and the Gilded Netherite Shield. Bastions can now generate in basalt deltas,
  and basalt columns stay out of them and out of the new structures. Blackstone mined without Silk
  Touch drops Cobbled Blackstone.
- Mystical Automation adds seven machines for Mystical Agriculture: the Infuser, the Crafter, the
  Farmer, the Fertilizer, the Infusion and Awakening Altarnators and the Enchanternator.
- Mekanism: Extra Modules adds three modules for the MekaSuit body armor. Phase Guard spends suit
  energy to block all damage, Chaos Anchor also stops the laser of the Chaos Guardian while Phase Guard
  runs, and Emergency Revival spends 10,000,000 FE of suit energy to cancel a death like a Totem of
  Undying. Phase Guard needs all four MekaSuit pieces and also blocks void damage, so if you fall out
  of the End you keep falling until the suit runs dry.
- Expanded Delight adds a cinnamon tree with its wood set, asparagus, sweet potatoes, chili peppers,
  peanuts and cranberries, a juicer, casks that turn milk into cheese wheels, salt and many dishes for
  Farmer's Delight.
- Create: Integrated Farming adds roosts that keep chickens and lay eggs, fishing nets for water and
  lava that also work on contraptions, and the Vacuum Harvester, which harvests and replants the crops
  around it. The Lava Fishing Net catches the fish of Lava Fishing. Create's Mechanical Harvester now
  also picks tomatoes, rice, mushroom colonies and flax, and a leashed mob no longer sits down on a
  seat by itself.
- Create: Dragons Plus comes with it and adds liquid dyes, dragon's breath as a fluid that pointed
  dripstone can multiply, the Fluid Hatch and four new kinds of fan processing: Bulk Coloring, Bulk
  Ending, Bulk Freezing and Bulk Sanding. They turn cobblestone into end stone and packed ice into
  blue ice, among other recipes.
- Actually Additions adds the Atomic Reconstructor and its lenses, the Empowerer and its crystals,
  laser relays for energy, items and fluids, canola oil power, crushers, drills, sacks, phantom blocks
  and many more machines and tools. The pack brings a SkyTekx build, 1.20.1-1.1.52-skytekx.1, with
  the content of the newer Actually Additions 1.3.26 on 1.20.1.

## Ender IO
- Ender IO is now a SkyTekx build, 6.3.0-skytekx.1, with features from the newer Ender IO 8 on top of
  the old version. Machines, conduits and items of your world stay as they are.
- New blocks: the Vat, which ferments hootch, rocket fuel, nutrient distillation and the other Ender
  IO fluids without power, the inhibitor obelisk, which blocks ender pearls and other teleports in its
  range, the relocator obelisk, which moves natural spawns of the mobs in its filter into its range,
  the attractor obelisk, which pulls mobs to it but leaves bosses alone, and the wireless charger with
  two antennas, which charges the items of players nearby.
- The powered spawner gains a capture mode, which fills empty soul vials with its soul instead of
  spawning, and the Mind Killer, which goes on top of a powered spawner and makes its mobs spawn
  without AI.
- New filters: the big item filter, the big advanced item filter and the limited item filter, which
  keeps a set amount in stock. Filters show their entries in the tooltip, and a filter in a conduit
  opens from the conduit screen.
- Energy and fluid conduits take an insert priority per side, redstone conduits can give a strong
  signal, and conduit facades cover a whole conduit bundle. AE2 facades work on conduits too.
- The travel staff and the electromagnet have keybinds, unbound at first, under the Ender IO heading
  of the controls. Both also work from a Curios charm slot.
- Fixed dupes with fluid tanks, stacked capacitor banks and ghost and filter slots. Machines no longer
  void their secondary outputs, and the coordinate selector no longer fails on the server when you use
  it without paper or aim it at the air.
- The wired charger only charges items while it has power. Before, a charger with a capacitor filled
  items for free.

## Create Crafts & Additions
- Create Crafts & Additions is now a SkyTekx build, 1.20.1-1.7.3-skytekx.1, with the fixes and
  content of the newer 1.7 line on top of the old version. Your machines, wires and items stay as
  they are.
- New blocks: the Electric Pump, a fluid pump that runs on FE, and the Electric Servo Motor, which
  turns a contraption to an angle set by redstone and works as a CC:Tweaked peripheral.
- Wires and redstone relays now carry energy across unloaded chunks, and shears cut all wires of a
  connector at once. Festive wire no longer joins two large connectors.
- The Tesla Coil can charge items in a Sequenced Assembly, turns gold items into electrum and
  Create's copper tiles back towards plain copper, and glows while powered.
- The alternator takes a shaft on both sides, the Capacitor stores FE, and the Electrum Amulet now
  charges the FE item in your other hand instead of giving Luck and Speed. Every charge shocks you
  for two seconds.
- One iron rod between two iron plates makes 24 spools.
- Fire spreads over oils and fuels such as seed oil, bioethanol and crude oil.
- Goggles show the burn time of the Liquid Blaze Burner and the charge time of the Tesla Coil,
  tooltips show stress values, and the Rolling Mill rolls paper into straw. New ponders explain the
  pump, the servo and the connectors.
- Fixed: the Modular Accumulator crash, long wires that vanished off screen, mined Electrum Blocks
  that dropped nothing, barbed wire that could not be waterlogged or wrenched, and missing death
  messages.

## Actually Additions
- The manual opens as a Patchouli book. Right click with it in the air or at a block that has no
  screen of its own.
- Black quartz ore generates in stone and deepslate, and wild canola, flax, coffee and rice grow in
  the Overworld. Villages can hold an Engineer house, and its Engineer villager works at the Coffee
  Maker and trades machines.
- Machines keep their energy, their fluids and their redstone mode when you break them. Machines in
  an Engineer house drop nothing.
- The Item Tag filter holds one item tag, set in its screen or by renaming it in an anvil at no
  cost, and every filter slot of the mod accepts it.
- The Crafting Table On A Stick opens with a key bind, unbound at first, under the Actually
  Additions heading of the controls, also from a Curios charm slot.
- Canola, coffee, flax and rice grow in the Garden Cloche of Immersive Engineering, and the Mekanism
  crusher turns a black quartz block into smooth black quartz.
- Bats drop wings, and nine kinds of vanilla chests, among them desert pyramids, Nether fortresses,
  End cities and woodland mansions, can hold crystals, drill cores, black quartz and bat wings. Four
  banner patterns come with their own items.
- Flux plugs and points do not connect to the Phantom Energyface.
- The Wings Of The Bats and the Storage Crate Keeper are still marked (WIP) and do nothing yet.

## Draconic Evolution
- SkyTekx DE Fixes, a new fix mod of the pack, brings fixes of the newer Draconic Evolution to its
  1.20.1 version, and the pack's scripts add two more. Server and client both need it, and the pack
  brings it.
- Fixed: the crafting dupe of a Draconium Chest that two players use at once, a crash with Chaos
  Crystal parts, and the insta kill damage of the Mob Grinder, the Chaos Guardian laser and a Chaos
  Crystal placed outside creative mode, which turned infinite with damage bonuses such as those of
  Apothic Attributes.
- The Draconic tools now rank above the Wyvern tools, with harvest level 128, mining speed 25, a
  damage bonus of 1.75 and enchantability 25. Before, they had harvest level 3.
- The Wyvern, Draconic and Chaotic tools count as pickaxes, axes, shovels, swords and hoes for other
  mods, and both staffs count as all of these but the hoe.
- The Chaotic Shield Capacity module takes an Awakened Core instead of a Chaotic Core.
- Squid, fish and other water animals drop souls at the chance of passive mobs instead of hostile
  ones.
- The Dislocator Receptacle and the Creative OP Capacitor block light, the button of the Mob Grinder
  says Claim XP, and a Chaos Implosion leaves a readable death message.
- Shift and scroll with an Advanced Dislocator still does nothing.

## Extreme Reactors and LittleTiles
- Extreme Reactors 2.0.97 and ZeroCore 2.1.48 replace 2.0.84 and 2.1.45. They bring the Energizer
  Active FE Power Port, power ports of up to 50 MFE/t, an on or off state that survives chunk unloads
  and a fix for a fuel rod crash. The pack gives the new power port the loot table that the mod lacks,
  so a mined port drops itself.
- Yellorium stays apart from uranium. The new deepslate yellorite ore, raw yellorium and the yellorium
  blocks stay out of the uranium tags, so the machines of other mods do not turn them into uranium.
- LittleTiles is a SkyTekx build, 1.6.0-pre161-skytekx.1, of the newest code of its 1.20 branch. The
  43 commits since the last 1.20.1 release bring fixes of the 1.21.1 line, a Chinese translation and
  fixes for two server crashes, one of them with tile blocks that hold no tiles.

## Bosses and food
- Apotheosis bosses can now be mobs of Iron's Spells 'n Spellbooks: the Archevoker, the Cryomancer,
  the Cultist, the Magehunter Vindicator, the Necromancer, the Priest and the Pyromancer in the
  Overworld, the Citadel Keeper and a Necromancer in the Nether and the Magehunter Vindicator in the
  End. About six of every ten Overworld bosses are now one of them. They come with raised health and
  enchanted gear, and the casters with more spell power.
- Three recipes of the newer Twilight's Flavors & Delight: four Shika Senbei from wheat, rice and
  liveroot, Moss Soup from a moss patch and a bowl in the Cooking Pot, and the Lich Trophy on the
  cutting board, which now gives Exanimate Essence at 50% instead of Transformation Powder.

## Smeltery and recipes
- Since 2026-11-05 the live server melts almost every armor piece, weapon, tool and item that holds a
  meltable material in the Tinkers smeltery, about 8,300 items more than before, including what
  SkyTekx Molten makes meltable. No item melts into more than its recipe takes.
- The items of the new mods melt as well: the machines, tools, drills, AIOTs and crystals of Actually
  Additions, the Vat, obelisks, wireless charger, Mind Killer and new filters of Ender IO, the
  machines of Mystical Automation, the MekaSuit modules, the Vacuum Harvester and the Lava Fishing
  Net, the Fluid Hatch, the Electric Pump and the Electric Servo Motor, and the juicer and casks of
  Expanded Delight.
- Drinks and jellies made in the Cooking Pot or the Juicer melt into the glass of their bottle or jar,
  and dragon's breath melts into the glass of its bottle.
- To close melting dupes, a few items melt into less than before: ironwood and its gear (45 mB iron
  and 5 mB gold per ingot), the enchanted golden apple (720 mB gold), the golden carrot and the
  glistering melon slice (5 mB gold), the scorched casting table and seared and scorched tinted
  glass. The Create Crafts & Additions items melt by the recipes of the new build, and its spools and
  electrum rod no longer melt.
- The Moss Soup of the Twilight Forest no longer melts into glass, and the Obsidian Ingot of
  Forbidden & Arcanus gives its 40 mB iron back again when it melts.
- The salt of Expanded Delight, of Pam's HarvestCraft and of Medieval Embroidery unifies to the
  Mekanism salt, every salt recipe takes all four salts, and EMI only shows the Mekanism salt. Salt
  that you already carry turns into it as well.
- The apple, sweet berry and glow berry juices and the berry jellies of Expanded Delight and of Pam's
  HarvestCraft fit the recipes of both mods.
- Cinnamon planks make only the cinnamon doors, fences, slabs and other wooden items, and no longer
  offer the oak ones next to them.

## On an existing world
- The structures, blocks and bastions of Bygone Nether only appear in Nether chunks that nobody has
  visited yet, so look beyond the explored area.
- The cinnamon trees, wild crops and salt ore of Expanded Delight only grow in new Overworld chunks.
- The black quartz ore, wild crops and Engineer houses of Actually Additions only appear in Overworld
  chunks that nobody has visited yet.
- An alternator with a shaft of another machine behind it joins that machine's network at the
  update. Check alternators whose back touches a shaft, because a speed clash breaks a block.
- Powered Tesla Coils now change gold items and Create's copper tiles that pass under them. Check
  belt and depot lines that carry these items under a coil.
- A wire that only one of its two connectors still knew about is removed at the update. Reconnect
  it with a spool.
