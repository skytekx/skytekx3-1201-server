## New mods
- Bygone Nether adds Catacombs, Piglin Manors and Citadels to the Nether, eight new mobs, withered
  blackstone, soul stone and the Gilded Netherite Shield. Bastions can now generate in basalt deltas,
  and mined blackstone drops Cobbled Blackstone.
- Mystical Automation adds seven machines for Mystical Agriculture: the Infuser, the Crafter, the
  Farmer, the Fertilizer, the Infusion and Awakening Altarnators and the Enchanternator.
- Mekanism: Extra Modules adds three modules for the MekaSuit body armor. Phase Guard spends suit
  energy to block all damage, Chaos Anchor also stops the laser of the Chaos Guardian while Phase Guard
  runs, and Emergency Revival spends suit energy to cancel one death like a Totem of Undying.
- Expanded Delight adds a cinnamon tree with its wood set, asparagus, sweet potatoes, chili peppers,
  peanuts and cranberries, a juicer, casks that turn milk into cheese wheels, salt and many dishes for
  Farmer's Delight.
- Create: Integrated Farming adds roosts that keep chickens and lay eggs, fishing nets for water and
  lava that also work on contraptions, and the Vacuum Harvester, which harvests and replants the crops
  around it. The Lava Fishing Net catches the fish of Lava Fishing.
- Create: Dragons Plus comes with it and adds liquid dyes, dragon's breath as a fluid that pointed
  dripstone can multiply, the Fluid Hatch and four new kinds of fan processing: Bulk Coloring, Bulk
  Ending, Bulk Freezing and Bulk Sanding. They turn cobblestone into end stone and packed ice into
  blue ice, among other recipes.

## Ender IO
- Ender IO is now a SkyTekx build, 6.3.0-skytekx.1, with features from the newer Ender IO 8 on top of
  the old version. Machines, conduits and items of your world stay as they are.
- New blocks: the Vat, which ferments hootch, rocket fuel, nutrient distillation and the other Ender
  IO fluids without power, the inhibitor obelisk, which blocks ender pearls and other teleports in its
  range, the relocator obelisk, which moves natural spawns of the mobs in its filter into its range, the attractor obelisk, which
  pulls mobs to it, and the wireless charger with two antennas, which charges the items of players
  nearby.
- The powered spawner gains a capture mode, which fills empty soul vials with its soul instead of
  spawning, and the Mind Killer, which goes on top of a powered spawner and makes its mobs spawn
  without AI.
- New filters: the big item filter, the big advanced item filter and the limited item filter, which
  keeps a set amount in stock. Filters show their entries in the tooltip, and a filter in a conduit
  opens from the conduit screen.
- Energy and fluid conduits take an insert priority per side, redstone conduits can give a strong
  signal, and conduit facades cover a whole conduit bundle. AE2 facades work on conduits too.
- The travel staff and the electromagnet have keybinds, unbound at first, under the Ender IO heading
  of the controls. Both also work from a Curios charm slot.
- Fixed dupes with fluid tanks, stacked capacitor banks and ghost and filter slots. Machines no longer
  void their secondary outputs.
- The wired charger only charges items while it has power. Before, a charger with a capacitor filled
  items for free.

## Create Crafts & Additions
- Create Crafts & Additions is now a SkyTekx build, 1.20.1-1.7.3-skytekx.1, with the fixes and
  content of the newer 1.7 line on top of the old version. Your machines, wires and items stay as
  they are.
- New blocks: the Electric Pump, a fluid pump that runs on FE, and the Electric Servo Motor, which
  turns a contraption to an angle set by redstone and works as a CC:Tweaked peripheral.
- Wires and redstone relays now carry energy across unloaded chunks, and shears cut all wires of a
  connector at once. Festive wire no longer joins two large connectors.
- The Tesla Coil can charge items in a Sequenced Assembly, turns gold items into electrum and
  Create's copper tiles back towards plain copper, and glows while powered.
- The alternator takes a shaft on both sides, the Capacitor stores FE, and the Electrum Amulet now
  charges the FE item in your other hand instead of giving Luck and Speed. Every charge shocks you
  for two seconds.
- Fire spreads over oils and fuels such as seed oil, bioethanol and crude oil.
- Goggles show the burn time of the Liquid Blaze Burner and the charge time of the Tesla Coil,
  tooltips show stress values, and the Rolling Mill rolls paper into straw. New ponders explain the
  pump, the servo and the connectors.
- Fixed: the Modular Accumulator crash, long wires that vanished off screen, mined Electrum Blocks
  that dropped nothing, barbed wire that could not be waterlogged or wrenched, and missing death
  messages.

## On an existing world
- The structures, blocks and bastions of Bygone Nether only appear in Nether chunks that nobody has
  visited yet, so look beyond the explored area.
- The cinnamon trees, wild crops and salt ore of Expanded Delight only grow in new Overworld chunks.
- The salt of Expanded Delight and of Pam's HarvestCraft unifies to the Mekanism salt.
- Server and client need all six mods, and the pack brings them. Joining with an older pack fails.
- Ender IO also needs the same build on server and client, and the pack brings it.
- Create Crafts & Additions also needs the same build on server and client, and the pack brings it.
- An alternator with a shaft of another machine behind it joins that machine's network at the
  update. Check alternators whose back touches a shaft, because a speed clash breaks a block.
- Powered Tesla Coils now change gold items and Create's copper tiles that pass under them. Check
  belt and depot lines that carry these items under a coil.
- A wire that only one of its two connectors still knew about is removed at the update. Reconnect
  it with a spool.
