Modrinth:        https://modrinth.com/mod/mystic-crown-compat

The addon is not shipped as its own jar. Its behaviour is re-implemented in the fork's own classes
instead of its five mixins (SkyTekx, 2026), ported from 1.21.1 NeoForge to 1.20.1 Forge 47 and Curios
5.x. Files that hold code derived from it (paths under src/main/java/twilightforest/):
- item/MysticCrownItem.java: initCapabilities, isWornBy, empowersScepters
- compat/curios/CuriosCompat.java: setupMysticCrownCapability, isMysticCrownEquipped
- item/TwilightWandItem.java: the extra Lich bomb and the Curios-aware crown check
- item/LifedrainScepterItem.java: the doubled drain and the Curios-aware crown check
- item/ZombieWandItem.java: the longer Strength, the crown gear and the Curios-aware crown check
- entity/monster/LoyalZombie.java: equipCrownGear, the CrownSummoned flag and its attack damage
- capabilities/shield/ShieldCapabilityHandler.java: checkLichCrownBonus
- events/EntityEvents.java: mysticCrownBlocksLichBombs
- TFConfig.java: the mysticCrownCurios and mysticCrownScepterBonuses options
- data/tags/compat/ModdedItemTagGenerator.java and data/curios/tags/items/head.json: the crown in the
  curios:head item tag

Changes against the addon:
- Curios 9 -> Curios 5.x (1.20.1) API; the curio comes from the crown's initCapabilities instead of
  NeoForge's RegisterCapabilitiesEvent, the curios:head item tag limits it to head slots, and the
  fork's CurioHeadRenderer draws it on the head.
- Right-click puts the crown into the vanilla helmet slot while that slot is empty, and into a Curios
  head slot only when a helmet is worn (the addon always uses the Curios slot).
- Lich bomb immunity cancels LivingAttackEvent instead of LivingDamageEvent.Pre.
- Crown-summoned loyal zombies are flagged in their own NBT (CrownSummoned) instead of persistent data,
  their gear never drops, and their held Knockback II stick applies its knockback.
- The addon's enableCuriosIntegration option became mysticCrownCurios; mysticCrownScepterBonuses is new
  and turns the scepter bonuses off for Twilight Forest 4.8 parity.
