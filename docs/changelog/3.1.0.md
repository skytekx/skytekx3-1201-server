**The Twilight Forest update.** SkyTekx3 now ships its own 1.20.1 build of The Twilight Forest with the
TF 4.8 content backported (`twilightforest-1.20.1-4.3.2508-skytekx.1`), plus a set of Twilight Forest
addons. This is also the first upload since 3.0.4, so the 3.0.5 Skylands changes are listed at the end.

## New in the Twilight Forest (TF 4.8 content on 1.20.1)
- The rebuilt Lich Tower (graveyard yard, wings, gallery, new boss room) and a reworked Lich fight with
  phases, clones, minions and candles. Alternative Twilight (Remastered), below, makes the Lich and the
  Naga harder still.
- Drying racks, jerky and other dried foods, Stale Bread, Moss Soup and leather tanning.
- Traveller's Gear: six armor pieces that take modifiers (double jump, sidestep, water walk, zoom, swap
  hotbar, auto repair and more), with an item display in the goggles.
- Traveller camps, hollow oaks, fallen trunks, berry and oreberry bushes, small lakes, Highlands
  Underground, and reworked Aurora Palaces and Naga Courtyards.
- Sinister Spawners, Mason Jars and jars with log lids, the Chiseled Canopy Bookshelf, Ominous Candles
  and candelabras, and the finished Ore Meter, potion flasks and magic paintings.

## New Twilight Forest addons
- Alternative Twilight (Remastered): harder bosses. The Lich's shield break summons a minion and puts the
  Blood Lust Curse on the player who broke it, phase 2 caps the damage it takes, and phase 3 powers it up
  and adds magic orbs. The Naga has 210 health, fires venom volleys, dazes players it hits from the front
  and chains its charges. It also adds nine spears and a Knight Phantom aurora.
- Spiced Twilight: the Maze Slime Beetle in the Labyrinth, Helmet Crabs that hide, kobold armor and
  babies, Fire Beetle Powder, Hydra Hide and the Cinder Pouch.
- Twilight Crossbow: Triple, Seeker, Ice and Ender Crossbows that fire TF arrows, plus ice and seeker
  fireworks.
- Twilight Mobs Loots: trophies for 48 regular TF mobs.
- Twilight Tweaks: re-fightable bosses (use Time Powder on a boss spawner's remains).
- The King of Twilight: the Twilight Lord, a final boss at the Final Castle.
- Twilight's Flavors & Delight with Farmer's Cutting: Twilight Forest: Farmer's Delight foods, TF knives,
  the Maze Stove, the Fiery Pot and cutting-board recipes for TF woods.
- Spartan Weaponry: Twilight Forest: ironwood, steeleaf, knightmetal and fiery Spartan weapons.
- Dungeons & Villages: villages, hedge dungeons and trading in the Twilight Forest.
- The Lost Blocks: towerwood and thorn building sets, stairs, slabs and walls.
- Twilight Treehouses: four treehouse dungeons in TF forests.
- Twilight Aether: Aether gloves and a cape in TF materials.
- Twilight Construct: eight TF materials for Tinkers' Construct.
- Apothic Category Compat (server only): Apotheosis loot categories for TF scepters and other modded
  weapons.

## Fixed and changed since 3.0.7
- Every mod in the pack is required. 3.0.7 marked 36 mods as optional, among them libraries like Cloth
  Config, JEI, Placebo and Balm, and an instance without them did not start.
- Sophisticated Backpacks and Sophisticated Core are gone. A port of the Backpack mod takes their place,
  and its sources ship in the `licenses` folder.
- Falling Meteors Reimagined, Elementary Staffs and BattleTowers are new. They download from GitHub
  releases.

## On an existing world
- Server and client must run the same Twilight Forest jar. Its network protocol is now `2-skytekx.18`
  (the stock 4.3 jar uses `2`), and Forge refuses the connection when they differ. Update the client
  with the pack and do not add another Twilight Forest jar.
- Structures that generated before the update keep their 4.3 layout, so an old Lich Tower stays the old
  tower. New structures and terrain appear only in chunks generated after the update.
- The "We Dine At Eternal Sundown" advancement (`twilightforest:twilight_dinner`) reopens if you
  already completed it. It now has 14 criteria (berries, berry medley, jerky, slime drop, moss soup),
  and completing it again gives its 75 XP and the challenge announcement a second time.
- Mason Jars in Twilight Treehouses chalets are empty. The addon's template stores the jar's item in
  the 1.21 format, which 1.20.1 does not read. This is cosmetic.
- Server operators: follow the upgrade steps in
  https://github.com/skytekx/skytekx3-1201-server/blob/master/okf/modpack/twilightforest-backport.md#changelog-for-players
  (old Twilight Forest jar out, new jars in, one Twilight Forest dimension reset recommended).

## Since 3.0.4 (3.0.5 was never uploaded)
- Skylands worldgen is plain vanilla datapacks now. Void columns get a featureless `skytekx3:sky_void`
  biome in which only the floating structures may start, so dungeons and villages no longer appear on
  the floor under the islands. The shared pack has no mod references; the mod content (Dungeons Arise sets, mod floaters,
  Botania features) moved to a Forge-only datapack, which now ships in the `.mrpack` too.
- Skylands travel works both ways: drop off an island to fall into the overworld, or climb or fly above
  Y 319 in the overworld to arrive in the Skylands.
- More floating structures, houses and villages in the Skylands; chocobos and gysahl greens appear only
  there.
- `skytekx-compat` 2.0.0 replaces the `skytekx-worldgen` mod and keeps only its Bird's Nests fix.
- Added The One Probe, SlashBlade: Resharped, Medieval Embroidery and The Aether: Genesis; removed Ruins.
