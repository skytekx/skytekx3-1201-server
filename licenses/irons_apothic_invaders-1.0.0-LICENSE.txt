The boss and gear set files in kubejs/data/irons_apothic_invaders/ are converted from Iron's Apothic
Invaders 1.0.0 by Raimu (https://modrinth.com/mod/irons-apothic-invaders, file
irons_apothic_invaders_1.0.0.jar, SHA-1 320585bf9ef2120545fbe481655cbb09650b7e15), a data-only mod for
Minecraft 1.21.1 and Apotheosis 8. Its mods.toml names Raimu as the author and says license="Unlicense".
Its Modrinth project lists the MIT License, and the jar carries the MIT permission text below as LICENSE,
unchanged and without a copyright line. SkyTekx converted the files in 2026 to the boss and gear set
formats of Apotheosis 7.4.8 for Minecraft 1.20.1 and changed some values, see the section Iron's Apothic
Invaders in okf/modpack/kubejs.md of the SkyTekx 3 repository.
